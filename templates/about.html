Our Story

From a small idea to your trusted cleaning partner

SparkleClean was founded with a simple yet powerful belief: everyone deserves to come home to a clean, comfortable space without sacrificing their valuable time. What started as a small team of passionate cleaners has grown into a trusted home services provider serving thousands of happy customers across the region.

We've built our reputation on three pillars: exceptional quality, reliable service, and genuine care for our customers and employees. Every SparkleClean professional is carefully selected, thoroughly trained, and dedicated to delivering results that exceed expectations every single time.
